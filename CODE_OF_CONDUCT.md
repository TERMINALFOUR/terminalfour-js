# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the
dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals, regardless of
characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, colour, age, physical
characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or
expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or
social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status.
The same privileges of participation are extended to everyone who participates
in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

## Encouraged Behaviours

1. Respecting the **purpose of our community**, our activities, and our ways of
   gathering.
2. Engaging **kindly and honestly** with others.
3. Respecting **different viewpoints** and experiences.
4. **Taking responsibility** for our actions and contributions.
5. Gracefully giving and accepting **constructive feedback**.
6. Committing to **repairing harm** when it occurs.
7. Behaving in other ways that promote and sustain the **well-being of our
   community**.

## Restricted Behaviours

Instances, threats, and promotion of the following are violations of this Code
of Conduct.

1. **Harassment.** Violating explicitly expressed boundaries, or continuing
   unnecessary personal attention after a clear request to stop.
2. **Character attacks.** Insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed
   at a person or group.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Characterising anyone's personality or
   behaviour on the basis of immutable identities or traits.
4. **Sexualisation.** Behaving in a way that would generally be considered
   inappropriately intimate in this context.
5. **Violating confidentiality.** Sharing or acting on someone's personal or
   private information without their permission.
6. **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
7. **Misleading identity.** Impersonating someone else, or evading enforcement
   action.
8. **Failing to credit sources.** Not properly crediting content you contribute
   — including undisclosed AI-generated content (see
   [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md#ai-assisted-contributions)).
9. **Promotional material.** Sharing marketing or commercial content outside the
   norms of the community.
10. Behaving in other ways that **threaten the well-being** of our community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, merge requests,
code review, discussions, and project-related communication — and when an
individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation to **terminalfour-sdk@terminalfour.com**. Include
what happened, where and when (a link if possible), and any supporting detail.

Not every conflict is a violation. Reports are taken seriously, investigated,
and handled as confidentially as the investigation allows. Enforcement is
carried out in private with the parties involved.

## Enforcement

Terminalfour Solutions Limited acts as moderator for this project. Depending on
severity and pattern, the response may escalate through:

1. **Warning** — a private written warning, with an opportunity to repair harm.
2. **Temporary restriction** — a time-limited cooldown, possibly limited to
   specific channels or interactions.
3. **Temporary suspension** — removal from project spaces, with conditions for
   return.
4. **Permanent ban** — removal of all access to project spaces, and rejection of
   current and future contributions.

Lower steps may be skipped for serious violations. This ladder is a guideline,
not a limit on moderator discretion.
